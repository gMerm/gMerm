PhD Student at the [Computer Engineering and Informatics Department (CEID)](https://www.ceid.upatras.gr/), University of Patras, Greece, under the supervision of Associate Professor [Panagiotis Hadjidoukas](https://www.ceid.upatras.gr/en/faculty/hadjidoukas) at the Laboratory of High-Performance Computing (HPCLab).
